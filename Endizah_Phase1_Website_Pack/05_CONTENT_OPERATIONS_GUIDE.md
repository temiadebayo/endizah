# 05 — Endizah Content Operations Guide

## 1. What the team can update
Through Sanity Studio, the Endizah team should be able to update:
- Homepage hero and sections.
- About/story content.
- Women and Men pages.
- Collections and their imagery.
- Garment showcase entries.
- Fabric descriptions.
- FAQs.
- Custom-order information.
- Contact details and social links.
- SEO titles, descriptions and social images.

## 2. Garment showcase content checklist
Before publishing:
- Accurate garment name.
- Correct audience and collection.
- Approved images.
- Accurate description.
- Verified fabric and fit.
- Correct colour/print description.
- Care information, if available.
- Correct enquiry destination.

Do not enter invented prices, stock quantities, sizes or availability statements.

## 3. Photography
For each garment, aim for:
1. Front view.
2. Back view.
3. Side/three-quarter view.
4. Fabric close-up.
5. Construction/detail image.
6. On-model image showing fit and length.

Keep lighting and colour accurate. Use consistent crops within a collection. Use descriptive filenames and useful alt text.

## 4. Homepage maintenance
- Keep hero campaign imagery current.
- Feature relevant collections.
- Remove expired announcements.
- Check CTAs after edits.
- Keep contact and social links accurate.
- Do not publish unapproved offers or delivery promises.

## 5. Custom enquiries
Suggested process:
1. Review incoming enquiry.
2. Confirm the request is understood.
3. Request missing details or measurements.
4. Check fabric availability and production capacity.
5. Confirm feasibility.
6. Discuss pricing and estimated timing.
7. Agree final terms directly with the customer.

A website enquiry is not a confirmed order.

## 6. Editorial quality
Before publishing:
- Is the information accurate?
- Are images approved?
- Is the writing consistent with Endizah's voice?
- Are all links correct?
- Does the page display properly on mobile?
- Is the content current?
